What it does

Built for the counter, not the demo

Everything a garment or retail shop touches in a day, in one keyboard-first app.

Voice AI billing

Say what the customer picked and the bill fills itself. Products, quantities and receipts, hands free.

You say“One Safa Noor lawn, and a Sapphire dupatta”

Find any item in seconds

Press F10, type “Alkaram”, and see on-hand quantity, retail and cost right there. Barcode scanners work too.

Stock and purchases

Live inventory with low-stock alerts, supplier purchases that update cost, and barcode labels you can print.

Staff-wise day close

Sales, profit and bills per staff member, combined at day close, with a cash count and a shareable Z-report.

Accounts that add up

Customer and supplier ledgers, profit and loss, and a clear line between cash profit and credit still to collect.

Your shop in your pocket

Check live sales, look up stock and see the day's close from a phone, on the shop Wi-Fi or from anywhere through a secure tunnel.

Offline first

Billing never waits for the internet. Data lives in a local database, with backups to Google Drive when you want them.

A day at the counter

From PIN to profit in four steps

Sign in

Pick your profile and enter a quick PIN. Owner, manager and sales roles see only what they should.

Find or say it

Search with F10 or speak the items. Quantity, retail and cost show as you go.

Bill and print

Cash, card, bank transfer or split. 80mm receipt, or send it on WhatsApp.

Close the day

See sales, profit and bills by staff member, reconcile the drawer, share the report.
