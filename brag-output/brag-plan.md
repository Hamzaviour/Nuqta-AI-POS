# Brag Plan: Nuqta AI POS

## What is this app?
Nuqta is an offline-first desktop point of sale for garment and retail shops: F-key billing, stock, purchases, accounts and staff-wise day close, with a phone portal on the shop's Wi-Fi.

## The angle
"Nuqta" is Urdu/Arabic for "point" and for the dot in calligraphy. The logo is a price-tag rhombus with one saffron dot landing at its corner. The video is one long point being made: every sale, to the point. Everything is fast, precise and small, like a dot.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Black screen. A single saffron dot drops in with a click. Type slams in: "Every sale, to the point." The full stop is the saffron dot.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The lockup: the price-tag mark assembles (blue tag rotates in, saffron point lands) and the wordmark `nuqta.` appears.
- Sign in: staff profile list, cursor picks a profile, four diamond PIN dots fill, "Open register".
- Billing on F10: the cashier types "Alkaram", results show qty on hand, retail and cost. Three lines land in the bill one by one, total counts up.
- Day close: staff-wise sales and profit cards arrive one by one, with a profit bar chart drawing in.

## Outro / punchline
Feature strip "Stock. Purchases. Accounts. Barcodes. Phone portal." then the lockup again with "Works offline." and "Every sale, to the point."

## User flow worth showing
Sign in with PIN -> F10 search "Alkaram" -> bill three items and total -> Day close with staff-wise profit.

## Tone
- Preset: polished (with app-store card reveals)
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a shop-floor tool
- Interpretation: few scenes, confident holds, small precise motion (no shake, no flash). Copy stays short and literal; the product does the talking.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 23.2s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #0B1024 (midnight ink), deeper #060918, app surface #F5F7FB
- Accent: cobalt #2F4BF5 (light #5B78FF), saffron #FFB224
- Text: #FFFFFF on dark, #0F172A on light
- Display font: Outfit
- Body font: Inter (numbers in JetBrains Mono)
- Strongest visual element: the tag-and-dot mark; diamond PIN dots; the saffron nuqta

## Share copy (draft)
Nuqta is a POS for shops that bill fast: F10 to find the item, one bill, staff-wise profit at day close. Works offline.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a warm bed
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-10 (109.96 BPM)
- Music treatment: starts at 0s, sits at about 0.5 volume, fades out over the last 2s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset read. Strong cues used: 15.82s (third KPI card), 18.55s (feature pills), 20.74s (final mark lands). KPI cards sit on beats 14.73 and 15.28; hold times stay above reading minimum.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use bass/RMS to breathe the ambient cobalt glow behind scenes. No waveform or equalizer visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse, motion-matched, low high-frequency-risk files
- Audio-coupled moments: search text typing (keypress ticks), PIN dot fills (click), bill lines landing (soft drop), KPI cards (card slide), logo landing (soft impact)
- Restraint rule: no SFX on every element; nothing bright or glitchy.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1: Hook — 3.2s
Black. Saffron dot drops to centre with a click. "Every sale," then "to the point." set in Outfit 800, the full stop saffron. Hold about 1.6s fully read.
Sequential/interaction: two lines arrive one after the other.
Audio intent: quiet anticipation, one click, one soft impact.
Audio-coupled idea: click on the dot; soft impact when line two lands.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 2

### Scene 2: Lockup — 2.6s
The dot becomes the saffron point of the mark; blue tag rotates and scales in around it. Wordmark `nuqta.` slides out to the right, descriptor "Retail & Wholesale POS" fades under it.
Sequential/interaction: mark then wordmark then descriptor.
Audio intent: the brand lands; warm.
Audio-coupled idea: soft impact when the point lands on the tag.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 3

### Scene 3: Sign in — 3.4s
The real login layout: dark brand panel left, staff list right ("Who's at the counter?"). Cursor picks "Hassan Raza — Sales Executive". Panel swaps to PIN; four diamond dots fill in saffron; "Open register".
Sequential/interaction: yes — cursor click, 4 PIN dots fill one by one.
Audio intent: precise, tactile.
Audio-coupled idea: click on select, 4 soft key clicks for the dots.
Transition mood: slide → Scene 4

### Scene 4: Billing — 6.0s
The POS screen. F10 chip lights. Search box types "Alkaram". Result rows show name, qty on hand, retail, cost. First row is picked; the bill gets three lines one by one (Alkaram 2-piece x2, Safa Noor lawn 3pc, Sapphire dupatta). Total counts to Rs. 8,450. "Print receipt" button presses.
Sequential/interaction: yes — typing, three bill lines, count-up, button press.
Audio intent: the satisfying rhythm of billing.
Audio-coupled idea: keypress ticks while typing; soft drop per bill line; small impact when total lands.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 5

### Scene 5: Day close — 4.6s
Dashboard-style day close: three cards land one by one: "Sales Rs. 186,400", "Profit Rs. 41,250", "Bills 63". Below, a staff-wise profit bar chart draws in for Ayesha, Hassan, Bilal. Label "Staff-wise profit, combined at day close".
Sequential/interaction: yes — 3 cards, 3 bars.
Audio intent: confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: card slide per card.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 6

### Scene 6: Everything else — 2.6s
Five chips arrive fast then hold: Stock, Purchases, Accounts, Barcodes, Phone portal. Line under: "Works offline."
Sequential/interaction: yes — 5 chips; hold full set for over 1s.
Audio intent: quick rhythm then rest.
Audio-coupled idea: none beyond a beat-snapped accent.
Transition mood: dramatic soft → Scene 7

### Scene 7: Outro — 2.6s
Lockup returns, centred, larger. Line: "Every sale, to the point." Music fades out.
Sequential/interaction: none
Audio intent: resolve.
Audio-coupled idea: final soft impact on the mark.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, clean
**Audio summary:** warm bed under a precise, tactile UI soundtrack, resolving on a soft final impact as the music fades.
